## English description

**Your father is dead. Parliament has named you acting president. In 26 weeks, the country will vote.**

The republic is young, short of money and caught between two much larger neighbors. Ministers bring you reports; the final decision is always yours. A cheap solution today may become a protest, a debt crisis or a problem at the border next month.

In this political management game, you decide which laws to prepare, whom to appoint, where to spend the budget and how far to trust either neighbor. You can try to defend democratic institutions, tighten your grip on power or keep both powers at arm's length. The country reacts to what you actually do.

### What you will manage

- **26 weeks until an early election.** Each week brings at least three reports. Some stories continue and return in a changed political situation.
- **A budget with real tradeoffs.** Education, healthcare, social support, the state apparatus, the army, debt and infrastructure compete for the same treasury.
- **A government you must work with.** Ministers open different laws, events and diplomatic options. Parliament can reject budgets and appointments.
- **Groups with their own interests.** The army, oligarchs, Church, unions and regions respond to policy and can change the rules of the game.
- **Two powerful neighbors.** Diplomatic concessions buy time and money, but can create lasting dependence. A hostile neighbor may invade.
- **Many possible endings.** Elections are only one way for a presidency to end.

### About this beta

This is a playable **26-week open beta** for Windows and Linux. It has a guided introduction, English and Russian interface options, seeded and random campaigns, and a single-save Cyborg Mode. The English story text is still being edited; reports about awkward wording, unclear rules and balance problems are especially useful.

The setting and characters are fictional. The game draws on political crises from recent history. Themes include war, repression, corruption and allegations of abuse.

**Made by dimazan10.** Third-party audio credits and licenses are listed in the game's Credits screen and the accompanying `SOUND_CREDITS.md`.

### How to play

Download the archive for your system and extract it. On Windows, run `Heir to the Republic.exe`. On Linux, run `HeirToTheRepublic.x86_64` after granting it execute permission. Godot is not required. Use **F11** or **F4** for fullscreen and **Esc** to leave an open panel. Begin with **Tutorial** if this is your first campaign.

Found a bug or a confusing decision? Leave a comment on this page. Tell me the campaign seed and the week shown in the game so I can reproduce it.

## Русское описание

**Ваш отец погиб. Парламент назначил вас временным президентом. Через 26 недель стране предстоят досрочные выборы.**

Республика молода, казна пуста, а два крупных соседа пытаются склонить её на свою сторону. Министры приносят доклады, но последнее слово всегда за вами. Вы решаете, какие законы готовить, кого назначать, куда направлять деньги и на какие уступки соглашаться. Сегодняшняя экономия может обернуться протестом, долговым кризисом или войной через несколько недель.

В открытой бете доступна 26-недельная кампания для Windows и Linux: минимум три события за ход, связанные сюжетные линии, парламент, группы влияния, армия, внешняя политика и разные концовки. Есть обучение, случайная игра, игра с сидом и режим киборга. Языки интерфейса — английский и русский; переключение находится в настройках.

Это вымышленный мир, вдохновлённый политическими кризисами последних десятилетий. В игре затрагиваются война, репрессии, коррупция и обвинения в насилии.

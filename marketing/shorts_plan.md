# TikTok / YouTube Shorts launch set

Use the same 1080 × 1920 master for both platforms. Keep captions on screen because many people watch without sound. Do not claim footage is final art: use actual game captures. Once the itch page is public, put its exact link in the profile and video description.

0–3 s: `shorts_card.png`. On-screen: **YOUR FATHER IS DEAD.**

3–7 s: first report screenshot, centered on the brother and the two choices. On-screen: **You are acting president.**

7–11 s: economy screenshot. On-screen: **The treasury is running out.**

11–15 s: foreign-policy screenshot. On-screen: **Two neighbors want your country.**

15–20 s: cover and CTA. On-screen: **26 weeks to the election. Free beta on itch.io.**

Caption (English): "Your father built the republic. Now you have 26 weeks to keep it alive. Heir to the Republic is a free political management beta for Windows and Linux. Link in profile. #indiegame #strategygame #itchio #gamedev"

## Video 2 — A budget dilemma (15–20 seconds)

Start with a close crop of the treasury forecast: **You need money before Monday.** Show two or three funding tiles. Then show a protest report or the weekly consequences. End: **The cheap choice has a price.**

Caption: "Every week brings three reports. Cut spending, borrow, bargain — then live with the consequences. Free beta: Heir to the Republic. Link in profile."

## Video 3 — Neutrality (15–20 seconds)

Start with the two neighbor relationship labels. Show an agreement that offers immediate cash but gives away a lasting resource or legal concession. End: **Can you stay neutral when both sides keep asking for more?**

Caption: "A young republic stands between two larger powers. Each offer helps today and changes tomorrow. Try the free beta of Heir to the Republic."

## Publishing order

1. Make the itch page public with both tested archives and 3–5 screenshots.
2. Upload Video 1, with the actual page link in the profile and descriptions.
3. Post Video 2 roughly 2–3 days later and Video 3 another 2–3 days later. Reply to specific questions with short gameplay clips instead of repeating the same trailer.
4. Note views, clicks and downloads after each video. If people watch but do not click, revise the call to action; if they click but do not download, revise the itch page's first screen.

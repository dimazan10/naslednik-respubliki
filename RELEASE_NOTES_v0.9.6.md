- Fixed mixed Russian and English text in budget cards and funding choices, including the Debt Service card, weekly amount, group reactions and parliamentary faction names.
- Improved English funding level names: reduced, standard and priority.
- Expanded the English UI audit to check budget drafts and funding choices.

Saves from v0.9.5 remain compatible.

Проверка английского интерфейса теперь охватывает черновик бюджета и выбор уровня расходов. Сохранения v0.9.5 совместимы.
